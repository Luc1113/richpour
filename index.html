A neighborhood café with a lot of personality.

We pour espresso, matcha and bright house lemonades, and plate fresh food to go with them, in a room that looks nothing like the coffee shop down the road.

Grab a coffee on the way to work, meet a friend for brunch, or settle in for the afternoon. Everything is made with good ingredients and a little extra care, one cup and one bite at a time.

What we pour and plate

  • Coffee

    Espresso drinks, pour-overs and signature lattes, hot or iced.

  • Matcha & tea

    Whisked matcha lattes and loose-leaf teas.

  • Lemonades & more

    House lemonades, juices, smoothies and seasonal drinks.

  • Breakfast & brunch

    Morning favorites, served all day on weekends.

  • Savory bites

    Sandwiches, wraps and salads made fresh every day.

  • Sweets

    Pastries, cakes and desserts to go with your drink.
